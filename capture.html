Decision factors — what to pick

  • Stealth & success rate (anti-bot resistance): choose Bright Data or Oxylabs (residential/mobile/ISP > datacenter).
  • Price-sensitive, moderate scale: choose Smartproxy/Decodo.
  • Highest throughput & low latency (less stealth needed): choose datacenter providers (IPRoyal, Webshare).
  • ISP/backbone routing (good mix of speed + residential characteristics): NetNut.
  • Enterprise features (SLAs, dedicated support, advanced targeting): Bright Data or Oxylabs.

Practical pros/cons (short)

  • Bright Data: Pros — unmatched targeting & scale; Cons — expensive, complex onboarding/KYC.
  • Oxylabs: Pros — reliable, enterprise-grade; Cons — pricey.
  • Smartproxy/Decodo: Pros — best value, simpler; Cons — smaller enterprise tooling.
  • NetNut: Pros — fast ISP paths; Cons — less granular targeting than Bright Data.
  • IPRoyal/Webshare: Pros — low cost, fast for bulk tasks; Cons — easier to detect, lower success on protected sites.

Integration & features checklist (use to evaluate any provider)

  • Protocols supported: HTTP(S), SOCKS5
  • Rotation modes: per-request vs sticky sessions
  • Geo targeting granularity: country / city / ASN / ZIP
  • Authentication: user:pass, IP allowlist, API keys
  • Billing model: pay-as-you-go (GB), pay-per-IP, monthly packages
  • Traffic expiry / non-expiring traffic
  • Rate limits, concurrency, concurrency tokens
  • Support & SLA (enterprise needs)
  • Free trial / money-back

Quick recommendation by use case

  • Large-scale, highest success (SERP, streaming, ad verification): Bright Data or Oxylabs.
  • Balanced cost + reliability: Smartproxy/Decodo.
  • Bulk fast crawling where stealth not required: IPRoyal / Webshare (datacenter).
  • Low-latency, ISP-like behavior: NetNut.
